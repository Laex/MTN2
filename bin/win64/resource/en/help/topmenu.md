## Top menu (F9)

**F9** or **Alt** opens the menu. Arrows move, **Enter** runs, **Esc** closes. The highlighted (accent) letter of an item runs it at once – in any keyboard layout: with a submenu open the letter is looked up among its items, otherwise among the categories. Item keys are shown to the right of the items. Items unavailable in the current mode are gray. A menu that does not fit in the window scrolls: a bar with ▲ ▼ marks shows on its right border, the selected item always stays in view, and the mouse wheel moves the selection.

**Shift+F9** (or "Find command..." in the ≡ menu) opens the command palette, a search over the menu items. Typing narrows the list: the words of the caption, of the category and common synonyms are matched ("shell" finds "Background console..."; "bc" finds it by the first letters of its words). The query shows in the field above the list; a word of the query may contain `?` (one character) and `*` (any run of characters). Arrows select, **Enter** runs, **Backspace** erases, **Esc** closes. Only the items available now are listed; the commands run last come first. The key can be changed in the keymap (command `CommandPalette`).
