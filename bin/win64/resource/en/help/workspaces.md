## Tabs and workspaces

**Panel tabs** – each side has its own. The **[+]** button after the last tab, or a double click on an empty spot of the tab row, creates a new one:

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+T | new tab |
| Ctrl+W | close the tab |
| Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab | next / previous panel tab (on a document or terminal – workspace) |

**Workspaces** – the tabs of the top row (**Ctrl+Shift+W** creates one, **Ctrl+Alt+PgDn** / **Ctrl+Alt+PgUp** switch). Each one holds its own pair of panels or a terminal. A double click on a tab renames it; the **[+]** button right after the last tab creates a new one.

**Saved workspaces** – **Ctrl+Shift+D** (list, jump, delete), **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+D** – save the current one.
