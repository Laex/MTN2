# Top menu (F9)

**F9** or **Alt** opens the menu. Arrows move, **Enter** runs, **Esc** closes. The highlighted (accent) letter of an item runs it at once — in any keyboard layout: with a submenu open the letter is looked up among its items, otherwise among the categories. Item keys are shown to the right of the items. Items unavailable in the current mode are gray.
