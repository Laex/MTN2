## Archives

- **Enter** on an archive opens it as a folder; **Ctrl+\\** or `..` in the archive root – leave it.
- **Ctrl+PgDn** opens a file as an archive whatever its extension: `.docx`, `.xlsx` or `.odt` (ZIP inside), or an archive with an unusual extension. The format is found from the file's content.
- **F5** from an archive extracts the selection, **F5** into an open archive adds to it.
- **Shift+F1** – pack the selection, **Shift+F2** – unpack.
- 7z and other formats – through the 7-Zip plugin (if installed). For encrypted archives, including ones with encrypted file names, the program asks for the password.
